How v2 trades

Every native BloFin timeframe can independently create a confirmed BUY/SELL marker. The bot evaluates that setup against horizon-level and whole-market structure, then may open a separate position. There is no daily trade-count limit and no cooldown; duplicate execution of the same closed-candle signal is still prevented. BloFin itself caps Multi-Position mode at 10 positions per instrument.
