How v2.3 trades

The bot uses one built-in aggressive profile and does not expose manual aggression sliders. Every enabled timeframe can independently create a permanent BUY/SELL candidate after its candle closes. Multi-position mode is automatic. While the bot is armed it also writes a performance report every three hours to logs/reports/, including trades opened/closed, wins/losses, realized P&L, win rate, profit factor, timeframe performance, execution rejections, current equity, open risk and the all-timeframe market state.
