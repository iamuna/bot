How v2.1 trades

Every enabled timeframe can independently create a permanent BUY/SELL candidate after its candle closes. Fifteen intervals come directly from BloFin; the other fifteen are constructed from native OHLCV bars and cannot signal until the constructed candle is complete. The engine summarizes Minutes, Hours, Days, Weeks and Months before calculating confluence so a swarm of correlated short timeframes does not automatically dominate the decision. There is no bot-imposed trade-count limit or cooldown; duplicate execution of the same closed-candle signal is still prevented.
